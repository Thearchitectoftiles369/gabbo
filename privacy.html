Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Gabbo lets two adults who want to talk about the same topic have a private text conversation. This policy explains what data we handle to run Gabbo, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. We have tried to keep it short and precise.

1. Who we are

Gabbo is operated by Yulian Momchilov (Bulgaria) ("we", "us"). For anything about your data, write to gabbosupport@gmail.com.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts, location, or photos. Gabbo does not use advertising IDs, analytics or tracking tools, and this website sets no cookies. The app keeps you signed in on your device by storing a sign-in token on it.

3. Conversations are private

We do not monitor or read conversations. We only look at messages that belong to a report. The other person never sees your email address or account ID, only what you write.

4. Why we may use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you asked for (account, matching, conversations).
  • Our legitimate interest in safety and security (reports, blocks, suspensions, preventing abuse and fraud).
  • Your consent (the waiting list). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to us.
  • Legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or hand over data.

We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you beyond the matching and the automatic safety suspension described in the Terms.

5. Who handles data for us

  • Supabase – database and sign-in (servers in Ireland, EU).
  • Google Cloud – runs the Gabbo servers (Belgium, EU).
  • GitHub – hosts this website.

These providers process data on our behalf. We may also disclose data to authorities when the law requires it or to protect someone from serious harm.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or stop certain processing, or move it. You can withdraw consent at any time. If you live in the EU/EEA or the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights; we do not sell or share personal data for advertising. To use any right, write to gabbosupport@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you, and we answer within one month.

To delete your account and its data, write to us from the email address of the account. Deleting it removes your account and the records linked to it; copies kept for a report may remain until their 90 days end.

7. Age

Gabbo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

8. Security

Connections to Gabbo are encrypted, access to our database is restricted, and the waiting list can be read only by our servers. No service is perfectly secure, so please do not share sensitive personal information with people you meet in a conversation.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for registered users, tell you in the app or by email before the change applies.
